18:T4ba,<p>Historically, Telugus used decimal system for writing integers, but used base 4 system for writing the fractions part.</p>
<p>This simple app helps you convert decimal part to telugu base 4 notation and the equivalent telugu descriptors.
For more, read <a href="https://blog.plover.com/math/telugu.html">this</a> fascinating blog post.</p>
<h2>How It Works</h2>
<p>The decimal part is broken down by base4 representation, so they all add up.</p>
<p>Based on whether a digit occurs in even or odd positions, they use different markings. Here are the different markings:</p>
<p>| Digit | Odd Position | Even Position |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| 0     | ౸            | ౦             |
| 1     | ౹            | ౼             |
| 2     | ౺            | ౽             |
| 3     | ౻            | ౾             |</p>
<p>With this system, you can encode fractional parts of numbers in a unique Telugu base 4 notation that has been used historically for measuring volumes and quantities.</p>
<p>This demonstrates the mathematical sophistication of traditional Telugu culture and their practical approach to representing fractional quantities in everyday commerce and measurement.</p>
