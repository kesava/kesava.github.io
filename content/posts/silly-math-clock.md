What's the time, you ask? Solve the following equations for it.

## A Different Way to Tell Time

This silly math clock displays the current time as mathematical equations. Each unit (hours, minutes, seconds) is represented as a simple mathematical expression that evaluates to the current value.

Instead of simply showing "3:45:22", the clock might display:
- Hours: `√9`
- Minutes: `45 × 1`
- Seconds: `11 × 2`

## Why?

Because sometimes the best ideas are the silliest ones. This project combines:
- Real-time clock functionality
- Mathematical expression generation
- A dash of absurdity

It's a reminder that programming can be playful, and that solving simple math problems to check the time is oddly satisfying (or infuriating, depending on your mood and math skills).

The implementation uses JavaScript to generate random but equivalent mathematical expressions for each time component, updating every second to keep you on your toes - and hopefully, on time.
