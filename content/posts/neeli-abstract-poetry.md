# Neeli

నీలి మేఘాలలో ధరణీ తేజం

నయనాంతరంగములలో వనధీ నాదం

This bit is abstract poetry. One interpretation is...

The glowing earth wrapped in dark clouds. Eyes deep in thought, lost in sound.

One might be an undefeated warrior, or an unrivaled hero.But can he really draw the bow of eyebrows on a lotus eyed girl? (Meaning: Can he make her calm down when she is angry)

He might command armies of thousands with a subtle sign, but can he really what's going in the heart of his queen.
