# Knife by Salman Rushdie

In this book, Salman meditates on the knife attack he faced, his recovery, his course, his health and the broader question of free speech.

He brings his usual wit, his powers of observation on mundane things of everyday life during a recovery and his words as sharp as a knife.

This short book once again reminds it's readers how Rushdie is still one of the sharpest minds of his generation.
