This is a silly swami name generator. Try a few ones to discover your spiritual alter ego.

## How It Works

The generator combines names from a comprehensive list of famous swamis, yogis, gurus, and spiritual leaders to create new, unique (and often hilarious) combinations. Each generated name maintains the gravitas of traditional spiritual nomenclature while being completely random.

You might become:
- Paramahansa Chinmayananda Prabhupada
- Vivekananda Maharishi Babaji
- Neem Karoli Ramakrishna

## The Inspiration

This playful project draws from the rich tradition of Indian spiritual leaders and their often elaborate names. While completely tongue-in-cheek, it's also a celebration of the diversity and depth of spiritual traditions in India.

The names are sourced from real historical figures - from Adi Shankara and Ramakrishna to modern-day gurus - ensuring that each random combination maintains an authentic ring to it, even if the result is absurd.

Try it yourself and discover your inner swami. Enlightenment not guaranteed, entertainment definitely is.
