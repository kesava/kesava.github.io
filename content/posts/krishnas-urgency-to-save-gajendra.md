# sirikin

```
సిరికిం జెప్పఁడు; శంఖ చక్ర యుగముం జేదోయి సంధింపఁ; డే
పరివారంబునుఁ జీరఁ డభ్రగపతిం బన్నింపఁ డాకర్ణికాం
తర ధమ్మిల్లముఁ జక్క నొత్తఁడు; వివాద ప్రోత్థిత శ్రీ కుచో
పరిచేలాంచలమైన వీడఁడు గజప్రాణావనోత్సాహియై
```

```
He has no time to tell Lakshmi,
No time to fetch his Shankam and Chakram,

No time to gather his courtiers,
Nor time to summon Garuda, his winged chariot of the skies.

His long, dark hair, slipping away unbound,
He can't bother to fix it;

Even the delicate folds of Lakshmi's saree,
Entangled in his own,
Remain untouched in his rush.

Such was his urgency,
Such was his boundless compassion,
To save Gajendra, in desperate need.

A call he could not ignore.
```
