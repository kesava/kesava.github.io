# Rama, the great

```
స చ సర్వగుణోపేత: కౌసల్యానన్దవర్ధన: .
సముద్ర ఇవ గామ్భీర్యే ధైర్యేణ హిమవానివ
```

```
He is endowed with every noble virtue.
He is the ever-growing joy of Kausalya's heart.
His demeanor is serene yet unfathomable, like the ocean.
His courage stands as tall as the Himalaya, unyielding.
```
