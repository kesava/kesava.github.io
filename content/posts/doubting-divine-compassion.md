# vinudata

```
వినుడట జీవుల మాటలు
చనుడట చన రాని చోట్లు

శరణార్థులకి ఓయ్ అనడట
పిలిచిన సర్వము కనుడట

సందేహమయ్యే కరుణా వార్థి
```

```
You are known
for hearing when people call for help.

You are known
for going where no one else can go.

You are known
to say, "I am here,"
when someone needs you.

You are known
to notice everything,
to watch everything,
when no one else does.

But now you are not here,
and I start to wonder
if any of it was true.
```
